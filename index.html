The Inside Out Playbook

A better way to grow your fanbase.

The old way: Build from the Outside In.

For the last decade, artists have built from the Outside In:

  • Chasing attention on every platform.
  • Creating for the algorithm.
  • Sending fans into platforms they don’t control, and hoping some of it turns into lasting fandom.
You don’t know your fans.

You can’t see who they are, what they care about, or how much they care.

You don’t know what worked.

You can’t see what all that time, money, and energy actually turned into.

You keep starting over.

Every campaign resets instead of building on the last.

This makes growth expensive, unreliable, and difficult to build on.

But growth isn’t just about reaching more people. It’s about creating more fans who care: more fans AND deeper fandom.

ListenersFansCore fansSuperfans

Fanbase value = More fans × Deeper fandom

The new way: Build from the Inside Out.

The strongest growth starts with the fans who already care. Give them reasons to go deeper, and let that deeper fandom create the gravity that brings more people into your world.

Here’s how you build from the Inside Out:

Instead of relying only on virality, create gravity.

When fans see they matter, fandom deepens. They return, participate and advocate. And when that passion becomes visible, it signals to everyone watching that something real is happening. New fans don’t just follow. They want to join. That’s Fan-Led Growth.

Overdeliver for the few.
Attract the many.

Step 1 of 3

Build your world.

An owned fan ecosystem4 plays

Give fans somewhere to join and go deeper.

A place you own, where you control the context, the distribution and the fan relationships. It becomes your center of gravity, where fans come closer and participate.

1

Give your fans a home.

Fan HQ is a home for your fans: website, link-in-bio, Posts, membership, and community in one place you own. Use it wherever you currently send fans.

2

Make it yours.

Set up your Brand Kit first. Fan HQ, landing pages and Posts all use it, so everything fans see looks and feels like you.

→→→

Now everything feels like you.

Your Fan HQ, pages and Posts inherit the same visual world.

Tap a control to switch it on or off.

3

Add your music, merch and tour dates.

Your music, tour dates, and merch in one place your fans can find. Add them to Fan HQ and Posts, and turn on your Posts feed so fans can browse everything you publish.

4

Let fans join as members.

Fans join as members, not followers. Every member gets a Fan Pass: free and paid levels with one identity fans carry across your world. When fans join, they give you their email, and their phone number if you ask, so you can reach them directly.

Step 2 of 3

Bring people into it.

A deeper understanding of every fan and how much they care4 plays

Know who your fans are and how much they care.

Every time someone discovers you, pull them into your world. Every join, click, purchase, and ticket connects to the same Fan Profile, so you can see who cares, how much, and what’s working.

1

Bring the fans you already have.

Import your fan and sales data, then connect streaming, ticketing, merch, and social, so the fans you already have and everything they do land on one fan record.

2

Acquire new fans.

Your Fan HQ, Landing Pages, sign-up forms, Instagram Auto DMs and QR codes. Landing Pages turn attention into fan relationships you can build on.

3

Onboard fans properly.

Choose what you ask fans when they join, then send a welcome email, so every new fan knows their signup worked and has somewhere to go next.

4

Learn more about every fan.

Use polls, quizzes, custom attributes and campaigns to learn more. Every stream, click, purchase, and join builds the Fan Profile, and Fanbase Analytics shows who cares, how much, and what’s working.

Step 3 of 3

Deliver better experiences.

A stronger, more valuable fanbase4 plays

Go to your fans first.

Put that relationship at the center of every new moment. Launch new music, tours, merch and updates directly to your fans. Give them access first. Recognize and reward the people who show up. Let them participate and interact around the moment, then bring the rest of the world in.

Fans first. Bring them in. Make it bigger. Every moment follows the same three beats with different tools.

  1. 1Fans firstPut the new single in a Post and gate it, so your members hear it first.
  2. 2Bring them inFans listen, watch the video, react and comment inside your world.
  3. 3Make it biggerOpen the Post to everyone. New listeners join to hear it.

Tools: Posts, First Listen, video, gating

You still released a single. Now the release also built your fanbase.

  1. 1Fans firstGive fans early access, a special product or a benefit.
  2. 2Bring them inLet them vote on the design.
  3. 3Make it biggerOpen the drop publicly.

Tools: Gate access, unique codes, store discount, polls

You still sold merch. Now buying becomes another expression of fandom you can recognize later.

  1. 1Fans firstRun a ticket unlock campaign that verifies real fans.
  2. 2Bring them inFans come through your world to unlock access, and you choose who gets priority.
  3. 3Make it biggerOpen the general onsale and invite new fans to join for next time.

Tools: Ticket unlocks, verified fans, priority access

You still sold tickets. Now the fans who care most got there first.

1

Reach fans directly.

Posts, email, SMS, and push, all from one place. Publish to your own feed and fan inboxes, where fans comment, react and share.

2

Choose who gets what.

Not every message is meant for every fan. Build audiences around who your fans are, what they do and how much they care, so the right fans get the right content at the right time.
