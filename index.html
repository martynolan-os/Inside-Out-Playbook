The Inside Out PlaybookOverview

Build from the Inside Out.

A better way to grow your fanbase.

The way artists grow today isn’t designed to build stronger, more valuable fanbases.

This playbook sets out a better model for growth, and how to run it. Three steps. Each one opens with the thinking, then gives you the playbook to put it to work.

Problem

Too much goes into chasing reach.
Too little goes into building fandom.

The dominant model for artist growth is built from the Outside In:

Compete for attention to chase reach.

Create for the algorithm.

And hope some of it turns into fandom.

Outside In

Post→Attention→Stream / Ticket / Store→?
New campaign→Start over ↺

Why it fails

Artists spend enormous resources chasing attention across platforms where they don’t control the context or the relationship.

Every post, release, and campaign has to compete for attention all over again. What worked yesterday means nothing tomorrow.

Even when artists do earn attention, they send fans right back out to someone else’s platform.

This means they:

  • Don’t know who their fans are, or how to reach them again.
  • Can’t see what their efforts actually created or what worked.
  • End up with streams and views, not fans who care and come back.

This makes growth expensive, unreliable, and difficult to build on.

The goal

Growth isn’t just about reaching more people.
It’s about creating more fans who care.

The artist-fan relationship is what actually creates value.

Reach matters. It’s how people discover you. But attention is not the end goal.

The goal is to create more fans who care. That’s what builds a stronger fanbase. More fans and deeper fandom.

MORE FANS →DEEPER FANDOM →

Fanbase value = More fans × Deeper fandom

And the strongest growth starts by creating better experiences for the fans who already care.

Fan HQ brings your website, link-in-bio, Posts, membership, community, music, merch and tours into one connected fan experience.

Fans can discover what’s happening, join as members, interact and go deeper.

You control the experience.

It looks and feels like you.

Complete your account setup so messages reach fans and data lands in the right place.

  • Add your social links. They populate your Fan HQ and landing pages automatically.
  • Configure your fan terms and privacy policy under Settings > Compliance before you collect fan data.

Connect Shopify to the Merch block

Connecting the Shopify Storefront API lets you embed products directly on your Fan HQ.

Step 1: create a Storefront API access token

  1. Open your Shopify Admin.
  2. Install the Headless channel from the Shopify App Store, then go to Sales channels > Headless.
  3. Click Create storefront.
  4. Under Manage API access, next to Storefront API, click Manage.
  5. In the Credentials card, copy the Public access token.

Step 2: connect your shop

  1. In Openstage, enter your Shop URL. For example, your-store.myshopify.com.
  2. Paste the Access token you copied in Step 1.
  3. If you share a Shopify store with other artists, enter your Vendor name. This filters products to yours only.
  4. Click Connect store.

Step 3: add a Merch block

Once connected, add a Merch block to your Fan HQ to start displaying products.

Fan Pass + membership

Every fan can join as a member with a Fan Pass.

The pass represents their relationship with you and can live in their wallet.

Over time, membership can mean more:

accessrecognitionexclusive contentexperiencesrewardsfree or paid membership levels

But don’t overcomplicate day one.

First, give people a way to belong.

The four stages of joining
  1. They join with their email. Every sign-up starts here, from your Join block or from unlocking a gated Post. Email and email marketing consent are always collected.
  2. They fill in their profile. Full name, username, location, phone number and date of birth are available. Click + Add Field for more, such as social handles or t-shirt size.
  3. They check their inbox and confirm their email address. If they stop here, you keep everything they provided up to that point.
  4. They come back verified. The link signs them in, presents any optional fields they skipped, and lands them back where they started.

Required fields do not block sign-up. They just have to be filled in before a fan can finish the profile step.
