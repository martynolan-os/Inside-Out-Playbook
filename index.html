The Inside Out PlaybookOverview

Build from the Inside Out.

A better way to grow your fanbase. 3 steps

Most artists have learned to grow from the outside in.

Chase attention.

Create for the algorithm.

Hope some of it turns into fandom.

There’s another way.

Start with your fans. Build a world they can join. Bring people into it. Then use what you know to deliver better experiences that give fans more reasons to go deeper.

Problem

Too much chasing attention.
Not enough building fandom.

Artists spend enormous time, money and energy competing for attention on platforms they don’t control.

And when they get it?

A fan listens here.

Buys there.

Gets tickets somewhere else.

The artist creates the demand, but someone else controls much of what happens next.

So the next release arrives. The next tour starts. And too often, you begin again.

The goal

Growth isn’t just about reaching more people.
It’s about creating more fans who care.

Reach matters.

It’s how people discover you.

But attention is the beginning of the relationship, not the end goal.

A stronger fanbase means two things:

More fans.
Deeper fandom.

Fans who join. Come back. Participate. Buy. Show up. Share. Bring other people with them.

That’s the asset you can keep building.

1.1How to

Start with one front door.

Your Fan HQ is your home for fans.

Use it as your link in bio. Send fans there from social. Send them there when you launch music, announce a show, drop merch, or simply have something to say.

Instead of scattering the relationship across disconnected destinations, give fans one place to come back to.

What is Fan HQ?

Fan HQ brings your website, link-in-bio, Posts, membership, community, music, merch and tours into one connected fan experience.

Fans can discover what’s happening, join as members, interact and go deeper.

You control the experience.

It looks and feels like you.

1.2How to

Choose your colors.

Choose your type.

Choose how buttons and cards feel.

Add your artwork.

Go as simple or as deep as you want.

How far should I take the branding?

Start simple.

Your Fan HQ does not need to become a six-week design project.

At minimum:

  • pick your brand colors
  • choose your typography
  • set button styling
  • add strong artist imagery

Then build from there.

The goal is not “customization.”

The goal is that fans know whose world they just entered.

3.1Principle

When you have something new, go to your fans first.

New song? Start with your fans.

New tour? Start with your fans.

Merch? Start with your fans.

Something worth saying? Start with your fans.

That doesn’t mean everything has to be exclusive.

It means the people who joined your world shouldn’t always hear about it exactly the same way, at exactly the same time, as everybody else.
